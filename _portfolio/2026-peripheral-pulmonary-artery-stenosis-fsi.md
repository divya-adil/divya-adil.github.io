I am developing patient-specific fluid-structure interaction simulations for peripheral pulmonary artery stenosis using patient-specific mechanical testing data. This project focuses on modeling pulmonary artery trees to better represent how local vessel mechanics and hemodynamics interact in diseased pulmonary vasculature.

By combining mechanical characterization with image-based cardiovascular simulation workflows, this work aims to support more realistic computational models for studying pulmonary artery stenosis and evaluating vascular behavior in patient-specific geometries.
